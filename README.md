# Philippine Railway Dynamics and Energy Optimization

A solo computational engineering project studying train longitudinal dynamics and energy use, beginning with a transparent one-dimensional model.

Phase 1 is research and model definition. LRT-2 is the provisional reference; vehicle parameters, route evidence, and validation limits must be established before implementation.
