# Draft technical data request — not submitted

Prepared 17 October 2026 for a possible LRTA/operator/OEM inquiry. This document is a research checklist and draft; no message or FOI request has been sent.

Subject: Public technical data for a single-train longitudinal dynamics study of the original LRT-2 four-car train

I am conducting a solo computational engineering study of station-to-station train dynamics and mechanical energy use. The initial historical reference is the original LRT-2 MC1–M1–M2–MC2 train documented in JICA's 2011 extension study. The study will distinguish published design specifications, assumptions and measured observations. I would appreciate existing public or releasable technical documents covering any of the following, preferably with document number, revision, date and applicable vehicle configuration.

1. **Traction:** wheel tractive effort versus speed, aggregate wheel power, short-time and continuous limits, reference passenger load, supply voltage and derating conditions. If only motor data are available, please identify motor and inverter counts, motor torque/speed curves, gear ratio, wheel diameter and transmission efficiency. Please distinguish motor shaft output from wheel output and electrical input.
2. **Service braking:** wheel brake force or achievable deceleration versus speed and load, load-compensation law, regenerative/friction blending, low-speed regenerative cutoff, command delay and jerk limitations. Please distinguish service and emergency specifications. JICA's Japanese Table 3.6-2 lists 4.68 and 5.40 km/h/s respectively at 7.0 standing passengers/m²; clarification of the speed range and test definition would be particularly useful.
3. **Resistance and inertia:** running-resistance coefficients with complete equation, units, whole-train/per-car basis, loading and tunnel/curve/wind scope; or coast-down records with grade, mass and confirmation of traction/brake state. Any equivalent rotating-mass factor or drivetrain inertia information would help separate running resistance from acceleration demand.
4. **One route section:** for Gilmore–Betty Go-Belmonte in one identified direction, rail chainages at stopping marks, rail elevations and grade/vertical-curve breakpoints, horizontal curve radii and spatial speed restrictions. Please identify the track, chainage convention, revisions and period of applicability. If another interstation section has more readily releasable complete data, that would also be useful. Rail-level data are needed because ground terrain elevations do not represent elevated or underground track.
5. **Validation observations:** an anonymized single-run timestamped speed/distance record, with sampling interval, units, approximate loading, train configuration, operating date and known restrictions. If possible, identify coasting/braking intervals. Independent runs should remain available for validation after any parameter fitting.
6. **Optional electrical extension:** pantograph-side imported/exported energy or voltage/current traces, auxiliary power under stated HVAC conditions, regenerative receptivity information, and the location and sign convention of each meter. These are secondary to the mechanical study; station/depot energy and train onboard energy should be distinguishable.

Existing reports, plots or tables would be sufficient; a bespoke dataset is not required. A public link or document reference is welcome where redistribution is restricted. If an item is unavailable, please indicate whether the corresponding value is a design rating, a test result, an estimate or not maintained.

Before an actual request, add the requester's contact details and choose the appropriate recipient/channel. Retain the original response and attachment metadata, and review applicability before promoting any values to model inputs.
